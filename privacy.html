Privacy Policy for ideashots

Last updated: November 28, 2026

ideashots is a personal micro-learning and news flashcard web app hosted at https://ominousblack.github.io/ideashots/.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google or email, we collect your email address and preferred username solely to authenticate your account. We also store your saved stashes, reading progress, personal notes, custom ideas, and topic interests.

How We Use & Store Your Data

Your account data is stored securely in Supabase (authenticated via Row-Level Security so only you can access your records) solely to sync your reading experience across your devices. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

Data Deletion & Contact

You may sign out at any time or request deletion of your account data by contacting the site owner via the GitHub repository.
